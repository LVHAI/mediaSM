Frequently Asked Questions

What makes MediaSM different from other compression apps?

MediaSM compresses photos and videos in one unified flow. You can select mixed media in a single grid, apply one Recipe that combines photo and video settings, and compress everything in one batch. Most apps only handle one media type at a time.

How do Recipes work?

A Recipe stores a combination of photo settings (quality, format) and video settings (resolution, bitrate, codec). Choose a Recipe before compressing, and it applies to all selected media at once. You can also open “Advanced override” to fine-tune each parameter manually.

Are my photos and videos uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely on your device. Your media never leaves your iPhone or iPad. MediaSM does not require an internet connection and does not collect any personal data.

Where do compressed files get saved?

Compressed files are saved directly to your Photos album. You can also save them into a new album from the result screen.

Why is the compressed file sometimes larger than the original?

Files that have already been heavily compressed may not shrink further. When this happens, MediaSM automatically keeps the original file so the output is never larger than the input.

How many files can I compress at once?

Free users can compress up to 3 files per day. Paid users can batch compress up to 20 files at once with unlimited daily usage.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap “Restore Purchase”. Your purchase will be restored automatically if you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

Does MediaSM work offline?

Yes. MediaSM works entirely offline. No internet connection is required for compression.

Which formats are supported?

Photos: HEIC, JPEG, PNG, WEBP. Videos: MP4, MOV, HEVC. Output formats include JPG, WEBP, MP4 with H.264 or H.265.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
